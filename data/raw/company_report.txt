ABC Technologies Annual Report 2026

ABC Technologies is an enterprise software company specializing in artificial intelligence and cloud solutions.

The company generated revenue of $850 million in 2026, compared with $720 million in 2025.

Net profit increased from $78 million in 2025 to $92 million in 2026.

The company's cloud business was the largest contributor to revenue growth. AI-powered enterprise products also experienced strong customer adoption.

The company identified increasing cloud infrastructure costs, cybersecurity threats, and competition in the artificial intelligence market as major business risks.

ABC Technologies employed approximately 8,500 people globally at the end of 2026.
